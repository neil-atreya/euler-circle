## Problem 28.2

$
(a + bi)^2 = i \\
a^2 - b^2 + 2abi = i \\
(a^2 - b^2) + (2ab)i = 0 + 1i \\
$

Equating the real and imaginary parts:

$
a^2 - b^2 = 0 \\
2ab = 1
$

Solving:

$
a = \pm b \\
2a^2 = 1 \\
a^2 = \frac{1}{2} \\
a = \pm\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\
b = \pm\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\
$
